# Analysis conventions

Carried forward from the registry study (see the bundle README) and extended for the new layers.

1. Stratify before reporting any modality effect. The pooled therapeutic-versus-diagnostic contrast in the registry study was a composition artefact.
2. Report a null as a failure to detect, with its power limit.
3. Check every superlative against the full table before writing it.
4. Regenerate derived claims about rankings, memberships and coordinates from the table in the same cell that writes the sentence.
5. A figure headline must not overgeneralise a stratum-specific result.
6. Never mix analysis bases between figures and text.
7. Every penetration or eligible-population figure carries a range, and the range is shown in the figure.
8. Every uptake curve derived from revenue names its price assumption and its calibration product.
9. Seed rows and snippet-only sources never enter a figure or an abstract.
10. Cross-country comparisons are reported on both per-capita and per-eligible denominators.
11. Projections are ranges under named scenarios, never point forecasts.
12. Every finding in a manuscript has a ledger id, and the ledger row names the script that regenerates it.
